How to download Instagram Reels on Android

On Android you only need your browser. Follow these steps in Chrome or any modern browser.

Steps

  1. Open the public Reel in Instagram, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open ReelGrab in Chrome, tap Paste, then Get download link.
  3. Tap Open download to load the video.
  4. Tap the three-dot menu on the video player and choose Download, or press and hold the video and pick Download video.

Where is the file saved?

Chrome saves it to your Downloads folder. Open the Files app, or tap the Chrome notification, to find it. Gallery apps usually show it under Downloads or Movies.

If the download fails

The Reel may be private, removed or restricted. See why a Reel won't download.

Permission

Respect the creator. Reposting someone else's Reel without permission can break copyright and Instagram's rules.
