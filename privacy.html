Privacy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What happens when you paste a link

Your browser sends the Reel URL to ReelGrab's server. The server forwards that URL to a third-party media-processing service, which retrieves the public Reel and returns a temporary video link. We show that link to you.

What we do not do

ReelGrab does not require an account or Instagram login, does not ask for passwords, and does not store the videos you request.

What may be recorded

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as request time and IP address) for security and operations. The third-party media service has its own processing and retention policies, which we do not control.

Your part

Do not submit passwords, private credentials or sensitive information. Only submit public Reel URLs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address provided by the site owner.
