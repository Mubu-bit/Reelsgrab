How to download Instagram Reels on iPhone

You can save a public Reel on an iPhone using only Safari. Here is the quickest way.

Steps

  1. In the Instagram app, open the public Reel, tap the paper-plane Share icon, then tap Copy link.
  2. Open ReelGrab in Safari and tap Paste, then Get download link.
  3. Tap Open download. The video opens in a new tab.
  4. Press and hold the video (or tap the Share icon in Safari) and choose Download Linked File or Save to Files.

Where does the video go?

Safari saves downloads to the Files app, in the Downloads folder. To move it to Photos, open it in Files, tap Share and choose Save Video.

If nothing happens

Make sure the Reel is public, then check the troubleshooting guide. Private browsing and content blockers can also interfere with downloads.

Permission

Only reuse Reels you made or have permission to use. Downloading does not give you ownership.
