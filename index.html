Instagram Reel downloader: what ReelGrab does

ReelGrab is a simple web tool that converts a public Instagram Reel link into a video you can save. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no password to share. It works in any modern browser.

How do I download an Instagram Reel?

Copy the Reel's link from Instagram, paste it into the box above, press Get download link, then open the link that appears and save the video from your browser.

Can I download Reels without an app?

Yes. ReelGrab runs entirely in your browser, so no extra app or extension is needed. See the step-by-step guides for iPhone and Android.

Can I download public Reels only?

Yes. ReelGrab cannot access private accounts, and it never asks for your Instagram login. If a Reel is private, deleted or restricted, the download will fail. See why a Reel won't download.

What happens to the link I paste?

The URL is sent from your browser to ReelGrab's server, then forwarded to a third-party media-processing service that retrieves the public video. The service returns a temporary video link, which we display. ReelGrab does not store the video. Full details are on the Privacy page.

Is it okay to download and reuse Reels?

Downloading for personal viewing is one thing; reposting is another. Reels belong to their creators. Reuse content only if you made it or have permission, and credit the creator where appropriate.

FAQ

Can I download any Reel?

No. Private accounts, deleted posts, age- or region-restricted Reels and unsupported links may fail. ReelGrab only works with public Reels.

Is ReelGrab free, and do I need an account?

You don't need to register or sign in to Instagram. Paste a public Reel link and get a download link.

Does ReelGrab store my videos?

No. ReelGrab doesn't save the video. It shows you a temporary link returned by a media-processing service. See the Privacy page for exactly what happens to the URL.

Can I repost a downloaded Reel?

Only if you own it or have the creator's permission. Downloading does not transfer copyright.

Is this Instagram?

No. ReelGrab is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram or Meta.
