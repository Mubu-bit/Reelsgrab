Instagram Reel won't download? Causes and fixes

Find the message you saw below, or work through the quick checks first.

Quick checks

  1. Copy the full link again from Instagram (Share › Copy link). It should contain instagram.com/reel/.
  2. Open the link in a private/incognito window. If Instagram asks you to log in, the Reel isn't public.
  3. Wait a minute and retry, then try a different public Reel to see whether the problem is only this one.

"That isn't a supported Reel link" / unsupported URL

Only Reel links work: /reel/…, /reels/…, or /username/reel/…. Profile pages, Stories, photo posts, and links from other sites aren't supported. Copy the link from the Reel's own Share menu.

Private Reel

ReelGrab can't access private accounts and never asks for your login. Nothing you try on ReelGrab will change that.

Deleted or unavailable Reel

If the post was removed, or is age-, region- or otherwise restricted, you'll usually see "No downloadable video was returned". Check that it still plays in a private window; if not, it can't be downloaded.

"The media service is busy" / provider failure

ReelGrab relies on a third-party service to look up the video. When it's overloaded or down, requests fail through no fault of the link. Wait a few minutes and try again.

"Took too long" / timeout

Lookups can take up to about 30 seconds. If it times out, retry once. Repeated timeouts usually mean the service is struggling or the Reel can't be retrieved.

"Too many requests" / rate limit

To prevent abuse, ReelGrab limits how many requests can come from one connection. Wait a minute and try again, and avoid repeatedly pressing the button.

Network problems

If you see "Couldn't reach ReelGrab", check your connection, turn off VPNs or content blockers that may interrupt requests, and reload the page.

The download link stopped working

The video link is temporary. Paste the Reel link again to get a fresh one, then save the video promptly.

Still stuck?

Platform tips: iPhone · Android. If a public Reel keeps failing, tell us via the contact page (without sending anything private).
