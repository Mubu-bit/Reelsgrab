Instagram Reel won't download? Common causes and fixes

If ReelGrab can't fetch a Reel, one of these reasons is almost always the cause.

Steps

  1. Check that the link looks like instagram.com/reel/… and is the full link.
  2. Open it in a private browser window. If you are asked to log in, the Reel is not public.
  3. Copy the link again from Instagram and retry.
  4. Wait a minute and try again, then try another Reel to rule out a one-off problem.

The account is private

ReelGrab only works with public Reels and never asks for your login. Private content cannot be downloaded.

The Reel was deleted or restricted

Removed posts and age- or region-restricted Reels usually can't be retrieved.

The link is wrong

Links to profiles, Stories or regular posts are not Reel links. Only /reel/ and /reels/ links are supported.

The download link expired

Video links returned by the media service are temporary. If the link stops working, paste the Reel link again to get a fresh one.

Temporary provider issues

The third-party service can be busy or briefly unavailable. Trying again later often solves it.
